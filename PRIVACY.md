# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

## Introduction

PDFOptimizer is designed with privacy in mind. We believe users should have control over their documents and understand how their data is handled.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when using PDFOptimizer.

## Local Processing

Many PDFOptimizer tools perform document processing directly within your web browser whenever possible.

For these tools:

- Files remain on your device during processing.
- Documents are not permanently uploaded or stored on our servers.
- Processing is completed locally using your browser.

## Server Processing

Some tools or features may require server-side processing due to technical limitations or functionality requirements.

When server-side processing is used:

- Files are processed only for the requested operation.
- Files are not permanently stored.
- Temporary processing data is removed after the operation is completed, unless otherwise stated.

## Personal Information

PDFOptimizer does not require users to create an account to use most tools.

Depending on the services used, limited technical information such as:

- Browser type
- Operating system
- Device information
- IP address
- Anonymous usage statistics

may be collected for security, analytics, performance monitoring, and abuse prevention.

## Cookies

PDFOptimizer may use cookies or similar technologies to:

- Remember user preferences.
- Improve website performance.
- Measure anonymous usage statistics.
- Enhance the user experience.

## Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on trusted third-party services, including analytics, content delivery networks, or other infrastructure providers.

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

## Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information and maintain the security of our services.

However, no system connected to the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

PDFOptimizer is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Changes become effective once published on this page.
