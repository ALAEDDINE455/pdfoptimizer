# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What is PDFOptimizer?

PDFOptimizer is an online platform that provides a wide range of PDF tools, including compression, conversion, merging, splitting, editing, and document optimization.

---

## Is PDFOptimizer free?

Many tools are available free of charge. Some features may have usage limits or additional options depending on the service provided.

---

## Do I need to install any software?

No. PDFOptimizer works directly in your web browser. No installation is required.

---

## Do I need to create an account?

Most tools can be used without creating an account.

---

## Are my PDF files secure?

Security and privacy are important priorities for PDFOptimizer.

Whenever supported by the tool, document processing is performed locally in your browser. If server-side processing is required for a specific feature, files are handled only for the requested operation and according to our Privacy Policy.

---

## Does PDFOptimizer permanently store my files?

PDFOptimizer is designed to minimize data retention.

For locally processed tools, files remain on your device. For server-processed features, files are handled only as needed to complete the requested operation.

---

## Which browsers are supported?

PDFOptimizer supports all modern browsers, including:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Brave
- Opera

For the best experience, use the latest version of your browser.

---

## Which devices are supported?

PDFOptimizer works on:

---

## Can I use PDFOptimizer on mobile?

Yes. The website is designed to work on both desktop and mobile devices.

File size limits may vary depending on the selected tool and technical requirements.
